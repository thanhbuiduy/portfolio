I'm Thanh. I build learning programmes for organisations - the kind where something actually changes after. I've done this at Samsung, Shopee, Dai Viet Group, and RMIT Vietnam.
I talk to the people who will be learning, not just their managers. I work with whatever budget exists. I design, deliver the programmes myself, so I know exactly what works and what doesn't.

Đào tạo không phải giải pháp cho mọi vấn đề. Tôi thường dành thời gian để hiểu rõ nhu cầu thay vì lập tức xây dựng chương trình. Đây là ví dụ về cách tôi phân tích với nội dung tôi nhận yêu cầu nhiều nhất - kỹ năng giao tiếp.
